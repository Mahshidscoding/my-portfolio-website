CommandFreak

CommandFreak is an XR application designed for smart glasses that provides real-time, interactive guidance for learning keyboard shortcuts. While the concept is built for smart glasses, it has been prototyped in both AR and VR to explore its full potential.

Project type Personal
Software Unity
Year 2024-25
Research

Problem

People struggle to remember keyboard shortcuts, slowing down their workflow. Most existing solutions require switching screens or memorizing lists, making learning inefficient.

Research

Insights

Users learn best when shortcuts appear in context, right when they need them. Many want a way to practice without disrupting their work.

Research

Target users

Designers, developers, and power users who rely on shortcuts but find them hard to learn or remember.

Branding

Inspired by FL Studio’s keyboard, the color palette uses soft pastels to highlight each key intuitively. The logo is designed for flexibility, making it easy to adapt and collaborate with other brands through logo swaps in campaigns and partnerships.

Phase one

AR Prototype

In the first phase, I developed an AR filter prototype for CommandFreak using TikTok Effect House to make it more accessible for users.

XR Prototype
